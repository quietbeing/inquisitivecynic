About Me

I'm a data and operations professional at Emory University, where I focus on fleet management, business analytics, and making complex mobility systems easier to understand and act on. I use tools like Tableau, Oracle SQL, and Excel to turn messy operational data into clear decisions.

Outside of work I coach my kids on life, cook obsessively, and spend too much time thinking about cars. The "Inquisitive Cynic" isn't pessimism — it's a commitment to asking better questions before accepting easy answers.
