The Perspective

Where Skepticism Meets Applied Intelligence

Twenty years of moving fleets, managing operations, and asking the questions nobody thought to ask. That is the foundation. What is being built on top of it is something different.

The InquisitiveCynic is not a brand built on certainty. It is built on the discipline of learning in public — studying how artificial intelligence and machine learning actually function in the messy, real-world context of institutional operations: transportation, parking, fleet management, and the human systems that hold them together.

The cynic part means nothing gets accepted on reputation alone. The inquisitive part means the questions keep coming regardless.
