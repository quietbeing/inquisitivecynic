The Perspective

An Operator Who Asks Better Questions

More than two decades leading operations — fleets, transportation, parking, and the budgets, teams, and systems behind them. The kind of work measured in cost-per-mile, uptime, and P&L, not slideware.

That operational core is the foundation of everything here: at Emory University, directing a 585-vehicle fleet across five locations, cutting total cost-per-mile nearly in half, and — earlier, leading campus parking — rebuilding visitor revenue from $2.1M to $5.1M. This is leadership learned inside the work, where the numbers are real and the consequences land on Monday.

The InquisitiveCynic is what that experience does next: applying disciplined skepticism, applied AI, and machine learning to make institutions run better. The cynic part means nothing gets accepted on reputation alone. The inquisitive part means the questions keep coming regardless.
